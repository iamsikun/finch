# Generic reading prompt (baseline 2)

Use this verbatim as the "good generic prompt" condition. It should be a fair, competent
prompt, not a straw man.

```text
Read the attached paper carefully. I am comfortable with economics, statistics, and
machine learning but unfamiliar with this literature. Explain:
1. the problem and why it is hard;
2. the main idea and why it works;
3. what the evidence or proofs establish, and under which assumptions;
4. how the paper relates to the most closely related prior work;
5. its main limitations.
Cite sections, tables, or theorems for key claims, and say when you are unsure.
```
